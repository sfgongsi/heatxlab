What's Covered

  • Induction heating system and coil failures under normal use
  • Battery manufacturing defects (capacity loss exceeding 30% within warranty)
  • Circuit board, controller, and charging port malfunctions
  • Device housing and structural integrity issues
  • LED display and button failures
  • Temperature sensor and heating control system errors

What's Not Covered

  • Damage from drops, impacts, or physical abuse
  • Water damage or liquid immersion beyond the device rating
  • Normal wear and tear (scratches, cosmetic fading)
  • Damage from unauthorized modifications, disassembly, or repair attempts
  • Consumable items (disposables, cartridges, pods) unless DOA
  • Loss, theft, or accidental damage
  • Damage from using incompatible chargers or accessories

↩️ Return Policy

30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

Not completely satisfied with your HEATX Lab device? Return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. We want you to love your purchase.

Return Eligibility

  • Device must be in resellable condition with all original packaging and accessories
  • Return request must be submitted within 30 days of delivery date
  • Consumable items (disposables, opened cartridges) are non-returnable for hygiene reasons unless defective
  • Engraved or customized items are not eligible for return unless defective
  • Sale items and clearance items may have different return terms

Return Process

  1. Request an RA Number: Email support@heatxlab.com with your order number, reason for return, and photos of the item and packaging.
  2. Receive Authorization: Our support team will review your request within 1-2 business days and issue a Return Authorization (RA) number if approved.
  3. Ship Back: Carefully package the item with the RA number clearly written on the outside. Use a trackable shipping method.
  4. Inspection & Refund: Once we receive and inspect the item (typically 2-3 business days), we'll process your refund to the original payment method.

Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days of receiving the returned item. Depending on your payment provider, it may take an additional 5-10 business days for the refund to appear in your account.

Shipping Costs

  • Defective or wrong item: We cover return shipping costs
  • Change of mind: Customer is responsible for return shipping
  • Original shipping fees: Non-refundable unless the return is due to our error

❓ After-Sales FAQ

How long does a warranty claim take?

Most warranty claims are resolved within 3-5 business days of receiving the device. Simple issues like charging port problems can often be diagnosed remotely without sending the device in.

Do I need to register my product for warranty?

No registration is required. Your order number or purchase receipt is sufficient to validate warranty coverage. The warranty period starts from the delivery date.

What if I bought from a reseller?

Warranty applies regardless of where you purchased. Contact us directly with your proof of purchase and we'll handle the claim. If you're a B2B partner's customer, you can also go through your seller.

Can I get service in my country?

We support customers worldwide. Most warranty and repair services are handled at our Shenzhen facility with international shipping. For high-volume regions, we work with local authorized service partners.

How do I track my repair status?

You'll receive email updates at each stage: received, diagnosis, repair in progress, and shipped back. You can also reply to your support ticket for status checks.

What if my device is out of warranty?

We still offer repair services at standard rates. In many cases, repair is more cost-effective than replacement. Contact us for a diagnostic quote — the $9.99 diagnostic fee is credited toward any repair you approve.

Do you offer accidental damage protection?

Yes! Our optional Premium Care plan covers accidental damage (drops, spills) for 12 or 24 months. Available at checkout for an additional fee.

How can B2B partners set up after-sales support?

Contact your account manager or email sales@heatxlab.com to discuss your after-sales needs. We can customize the program to match your business model and volume.
